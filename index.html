What does Nexus Consultant do?

Nexus Consultant is a financial and advisory consultancy in Mombasa, Kenya. We provide accounting and bookkeeping, tax planning and KRA compliance, company registration and secretarial services, and strategic financial advisory, all from one dedicated team.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Straight answers about our accounting, tax, registration and advisory services.

    What services does Nexus Consultant offer?

    Nexus Consultant offers four services: accounting and bookkeeping, tax planning and compliance, company registration and secretarial services, and strategic financial advisory.

    Who does Nexus Consultant work with?

    We work with sole proprietors, small and medium enterprises (SMEs) and larger enterprises. Our package finder recommends a Starter, Growth or Enterprise Partner package based on your business size and the services you need.

    What does accounting and bookkeeping include?

    It covers daily transaction recording, monthly profit and loss statements, balance sheets, payroll management and custom reporting, so your records stay organised and audit-ready.

    Can you help with KRA tax filing and compliance?

    Yes. Our tax service covers personal and corporate tax planning, KRA iTax filing, VAT and withholding tax, tax health checks and penalty prevention.

    Can you help me register a company in Kenya?

    Yes. We assist with business name search, BN2 and Private Limited registration, KRA PIN generation, business permits and CR12 assistance.

    What does strategic financial advisory cover?

    It covers cash flow forecasting, budgeting, investment evaluation, capital structure guidance and profitability optimisation, explained in plain language.

    What is included in each package?

    Starter (sole proprietors): simple monthly bookkeeping and KRA iTax filing support. Growth (SMEs): full bookkeeping with a monthly profit and loss statement, VAT and withholding tax returns, and a quarterly review with your advisor. Enterprise Partner: a dedicated finance team, audit-ready reporting and payroll, and monthly advisory with a compliance calendar. Prices are not listed on this website, so please request a quote.

    How do I get started with Nexus Consultant?

    Book a free strategy call using the form on this page, call +254 701 269 546, or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within one business day.

    Where is Nexus Consultant located and when is it open?

    Nexus Consultant is based in Mombasa, Kenya. Opening hours are Monday to Friday 8:00 to 17:00 and Saturday 9:00 to 13:00.
